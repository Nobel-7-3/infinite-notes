Everything on one page

Stop juggling six apps
to revise one topic.

A PDF viewer, a video player, a flashcard app, a quiz, a notebook. Infinite Notes puts them on the same canvas — and lets you write over all of it.

Writing that feels like paper

Pen, pencil, fountain pen, marker and highlighter — pressure and tilt aware, with palm rejection so you can rest your hand. Study tape hides answers; a laser pointer helps you present.

Every lecture, one page

Import dozens of PDFs at once and they lay out in tidy rows. Drop in a Word document and it arrives as pages. Slides stay locked in place while you write over them, and two notes merge into one without covering a line.

NotebookLM, alive on the canvas

Nine kinds of study material, every one interactive — and every one you can write over. Videos play while your ink sits on top. Mind maps drag and fold. Flashcards flip. Quizzes mark your answer and show why.

Lecture capture EARLY ACCESS

Privacy

Your notes never touch our servers — because there aren't any.

No accountOpen the app and write. There is nothing to sign up for.
No analytics, no adsWe collect nothing. The one outside party is Google's on-device handwriting recogniser, which reports anonymous performance metrics to Google — never your notes. Details

Infinite Notes for Android

The full app. Everything works offline.

Download the APK
  1. Open the downloaded file on your tablet.
  2. Allow installs from that app when Android asks, then tap Install.
  3. Updating later? Install over the top — your notes stay.
Windows 10 / 11

Desktop companion

Your notes on a big screen, synced over USB.

Download for Windows
  1. Run the installer. SmartScreen may warn — More info → Run anyway.
  2. Open Infinite Notes from the Start menu.
  3. Plug in your tablet and tap Sync.
